# ZPM: fusion and Stargate

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p13` · **Era:** [ZPM &amp; Classic Stargate](../eras/zpm.md)

Fusion MK I, then MK II with argon plasma power and tritanium, the era multiblocks and the Classic Stargate. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 13 days from ZPM to the Classic Stargate (3 runs, 12 to 13).

<a id="fusion2"></a>

## Fusion MK II (darmstadtium)

- **Module key:** `plan:fusion2` · step 73
- **Chain:** [Fusion MK II (ZPM): URD superconducting coil → fusion coil, advanced reflector casing, then the reactor (needs 4 UV circuits) and darmstadtium](../chains/zpm/zpm_fusion.md); also [ZPM power: argon plasma in Fusion MK II (carbon + magnesium) into Large Plasma Turbines](../chains/zpm/zpm_argon.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:uranium_rhodium_dinaquadide_ingot`, `gtceu:trinium_ingot`, `gtceu:naquadria_ingot`, `gtceu:zpm_machine_hull`, `gtceu:zpm_electric_motor`, `gtceu:research_station`
- **Makes:** `start_core:zpm_fusion_reactor`, `gtceu:darmstadtium_bucket`, `gtceu:darmstadtium_ingot`, `gtceu:argon_plasma_bucket`, `gtceu:tritanium_ingot`
- **Power:** buffer up to 320 MEU; argon plasma 24 576 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - MK II needs station research, a URD superconducting coil and the advanced reflector casing; the reactor takes 4 UV circuits.
    - Darmstadtium is the UV hull metal.
    - Buffers (start\_core lang): MK I 160 MEU, MK II 320 MEU, MK III 640 MEU.
    - Argon plasma (zpm\_argon): reflector T2, 144 mB carbon + 144 mB magnesium → 1000 mB, 24 576 EU/t, 7.2 s, 180 MEU start: the ZPM power players actually use.
    - Tritanium does not need MK III: reflector T3 (complex reflector casing), 30 720 EU/t, 21.6 s, 200 MEU start, fits MK II. Neutronium (600 MEU start) does need MK III.
    - A reflector above the recipe tier raises the overclock cap by the tier difference; each overclock doubles EU/t and halves time; recipes with a higher minimum reflector tier do not run.

## Era multiblocks and the Classic Stargate

- **Module key:** `plan:zpmmultis` · step 74
- **Chain:** [Era multiblocks 1: Component Part Assembly, Heat Chamber, Large Rotor Machine (Assembly Line, ZPM power, 4 UV circuits each, station research 16 CWU/t)](../chains/zpm/zpm_multis.md); also [Era multiblocks 2: Runic Engraver (RCAS), Stargate Component Assembly, Dimensional Finder with coordinate crystals](../chains/zpm/zpm_multis2.md), [Stargate rods: rod base → raw rod (Large Rotor Machine, foil reams, lubricant) → activated rod (Heat Chamber, nether star concentrate)](../chains/zpm/zpm_rods.md), [Classic Stargate: runes (RCAS) + gate parts (Component Part Assembly) + activated rods → ring, chevron, base blocks (Stargate Component Assembly) → Create mechanical crafting](../chains/zpm/zpm_gate.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:zpm_robot_arm`, `gtceu:zpm_electric_motor`, `gtceu:zpm_machine_hull`, `gtceu:crystal_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:research_station`, `gtceu:dragonsteel_ingot`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:component_part_assembly`, `gtceu:heat_chamber`, `gtceu:large_rotor_machine`, `gtceu:runic_circuitry_assembling_station`, `gtceu:stargate_component_assembly`, `sgjourney:classic_stargate`, `sgjourney:classic_stargate_base_block`
- **Watch out:**
    - Each controller is an Assembly Line recipe with 4 UV circuits and station research (16 CWU/t).
    - Rods and gate parts are UV-power recipes: a LuV 16A hatch gives UV speed (quest).
    - The whole gate needs 24 ring blocks and 484 activated rods.
    - **Unverified** foil ream recipe.
    - The Classic Stargate is the ZPM → UV step: its bars, ring, chevrons and base (Stargate Component Assembly) are UV-voltage recipes. Without a UV hatch, 4 A of ZPM (two 2 A ZPM hatches) counts as 1 A at UV (GT amperage rule, verified for MV/HV).
