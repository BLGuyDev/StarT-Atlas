## Engine wall: 208 Huge Diesel Engines on biodiesel

- **Module key:** `plan:cb_diesel` · step 100 · combo for era `lv`
- **Chain:** [Combo: a wall of Huge Diesel Engines on crude biodiesel (Create)](../chains/combos/combo_diesel.md)
- **Uses:** `create_new_age:generator_coil`
- **Makes:** `createdieselgenerators:huge_diesel_engine`, `createdieselgenerators:biodiesel_bucket`
- **Power:** 12 288 SU per engine at 224 RPM; 208 engines = 2 555 904 SU
- **Watch out:**
    - **Verified** 208 x 12 288 SU = 2 555 904 SU on crude biodiesel (Create mixing 100 plant oil + 100 ethanol → 200).
    - **Unverified** the real fuel use in game and the plant oil source; the GT fish oil biodiesel route needs the IV Industrial Fishery.

<a id="cb_quad"></a>

## Quad: four multiblocks sharing walls and hatches

- **Module key:** `plan:cb_quad` · step 101 · combo for era `hv`
- **Chain:** [Combo: a quad of multiblocks sharing walls and hatches](../chains/combos/combo_quad.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:cupronickel_coil_block`, `gtceu:hv_machine_hull`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:auto_maintenance_hatch`
- **Watch out:**
    - Shareable: energy hatches, buses, fluid hatches, casings, coils. Never shared: maintenance, parallel, laser, rotor holder, HPCA and optical parts.
    - A shared energy hatch feeds two machines: give it 2 A. Two 2 A MV hatches (4 A) reach HV recipes.

<a id="cb_cubes"></a>

## Perfect-OC cubes from the Multiblock Upgrade Kit

- **Module key:** `plan:cb_cubes` · step 102 · combo for era `hv`
- **Chain:** [Combo: Perfect-OC cubes (Multiblock Upgrade Kit) built as a quad](../chains/combos/combo_cubes.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:micro_processor_computer`, `gtceu:hv_machine_hull`
- **Makes:** `kubejs:multiblock_upgrade_kit`
- **Power:** kit: HV assembler; cube: one 2 A energy hatch
- **Watch out:**
    - The kit needs an EV circuit: an end-of-HV build.
    - 3x3x3 hollow cube: at least 5 own casings, exactly one 2 A energy hatch (4 A does not count) and one maintenance hatch, air behind the controller; perfect overclock + batch, no parallel.

## Assembler Matrix and pattern buffers (AE2)

- **Module key:** `plan:cb_ae2` · step 103 · combo for era `ev`
- **Chain:** [Combo: Assembler Matrix and pattern buffers (AE2 automation)](../chains/combos/combo_ae2.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:ruthenium_dust`, `gtceu:cleanroom_glass`, `gtceu:micro_processor_computer`
- **Makes:** `expatternprovider:assembler_matrix_frame`
- **Power:** EV assembler 1 920 EU/t per piece
- **Watch out:**
    - All matrix pieces are EV assembler recipes; ruthenium (platinum line) and plascrete (cleanroom) are the gates; the pattern / crafter / speed blocks need an IV-voltage alloy.
    - Smart blocking comes from ExpandedAE, not plain AE2.

## Extreme Chemical Reactor with a parallel hatch

- **Module key:** `plan:cb_ecr` · step 104 · combo for era `luv`
- **Chain:** [Combo: Extreme Chemical Reactor with a parallel hatch](../chains/combos/combo_ecr.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:micro_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:iv_emitter`, `gtceu:naquadah_ingot`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:extreme_chemical_reactor`
- **Watch out:**

## Modular Combustion Frame with SCM / SRM modules

- **Module key:** `plan:cb_mcf` · step 105 · combo for era `zpm`
- **Chain:** [Combo: Modular Combustion Frame with combustion and rocket modules](../chains/combos/combo_mcf.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:luv_machine_hull`, `gtceu:zpm_machine_hull`, `gtceu:quantum_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:research_station`
