# Raw materials

Every basic ore, gem and fluid from cobblestone and water. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 2.5 days from the start to MV in total (median of 4 runs).

<a id="sieves"></a>

## Sieve block (4 sieves)

- **Module key:** `plan:sieves` · step 8
- **Chain:** [Four-sieve block (gravel, sand, dust, blackstone)](../chains/lv/sieve_block4.md); also [Infinite gravel (Create)](../chains/steam/inf_gravel_create.md), [Gravel, sand &amp; dust: Steam Forge Hammer](../chains/steam/gravel_steam.md)
- **Makes:** `gtceu:crushed_iron_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_copper_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_tin_ore`, `minecraft:diamond`, `minecraft:redstone`, `gtceu:sulfur_dust`, `gtceu:crushed_galena_ore`, `minecraft:cobblestone`, `minecraft:sand`, `gtceu:crushed_sphalerite_ore`
- **Stock:** `minecraft:gravel` in 2k buffer
- **Watch out:**
    - Cobblestone comes from the 4 stone barrels at the start of the block; the forge hammers turn it into gravel, then sand, then dust.
    - Spare cobblestone and sand can be piped to the obsidian and rubber lines.
    - Buffer drawer before every sieve.
    - Mesh in the input bus is never used up.
    - Plan the floor for 4 sieves side by side.

## Smelting: ingots and wrought iron

- **Module key:** `plan:ingots` · step 11
- **Chain:** [Ingots, wrought iron, wires and foil from the sieve](../chains/lv/ingots.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:crushed_iron_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_copper_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_tin_ore`
- **Makes:** `minecraft:iron_ingot`, `minecraft:copper_ingot`, `gtceu:tin_ingot`, `gtceu:wrought_iron_ingot`, `gtceu:wrought_iron_plate`, `gtceu:copper_foil`

<a id="soul_infused"></a>

## Soul infused ingots (LV superconductor)

- **Module key:** `plan:soul_infused` · step 13
- **Chain:** [Soul infused ingots (LV superconductor): sand → soul sand → dust + invar](../chains/lv/soul_infused.md)
- **Uses:** `minecraft:sand`, `exnihilosequentia:witch_water_bucket`, `gtceu:iron_dust`, `gtceu:nickel_dust`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:soul_infused_ingot`, `thermal_extra:soul_sand_dust`, `gtceu:invar_ingot`, `minecraft:soul_sand`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:soul_infused_ingot` in 64, `thermal_extra:soul_sand_dust` in 64, `gtceu:invar_ingot` in 64
- **Power:** about 8 to 16 EU/t per machine (macerator 8, soul smelter 8, invar smelter 16), under 60 EU/t in total
- **Watch out:**
    - One soul infused Alloy Smelter run: 1 invar (ingot or dust) + 2 thermal\_extra:soul\_sand\_dust → 3 ingots in 10 s at 8 EU/t = 18 ingots a minute.
    - Soul sand needs no Nether: sand + 250 mB witch water in a Large Barrel (no spores in it) every 5 ticks; sifting soul sand is removed.
    - First nickel comes from the electric ore factory (iron ore gives 45 % nickel dust), not the Void Extractor, which itself needs soul infused hex wire.
    - Needed soon: LV Energy Converter wire, Void Extractor, soul infused casing, plates for Functional Storage void upgrades.

## Void Extractor

- **Module key:** `plan:void` · step 16
- **Chain:** [Void Extractor](../chains/lv/void.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:drilling_fluid_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:raw_coal`, `minecraft:raw_gold`, `gtceu:raw_silver`, `gtceu:raw_sodalite`, `gtceu:raw_pentlandite`, `gtceu:raw_realgar`, `gtceu:rare_ore_residue_bucket`, `gtceu:raw_ore_slurry_bucket`
