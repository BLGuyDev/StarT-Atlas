# IV: tungsten, nether star, IV circuits and power

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p10` · **Era:** [IV](../eras/iv.md)

Tungsten steel, the nether star loop, the first IV circuit, IV power and the IV machine parts. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 2 days in IV (only 2 runs, range 1 to 3).

<a id="tungsten"></a>

## Tungsten and tungsten steel

- **Module key:** `plan:tungsten` · step 55
- **Chain:** [Tungsten and tungsten steel: chemical bath → electrolyzer → EBF 3600 K (nichrome coils) → Vacuum Freezer](../chains/iv/tungsten.md); also [Alloy Blast Smelter (alloy dust → molten metal in one step) and the Super EBF](../chains/iv/abs_super_ebf.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:steel_ingot`, `gtceu:hydrochloric_acid_bucket`, `gtceu:helium_bucket`, `gtceu:nichrome_coil_block`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:tungsten_ingot`, `gtceu:tungsten_steel_ingot`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:tungsten_steel_ingot` in 64
- **Power:** EV chemical bath and electrolyzer 960 EU/t; EBF 1920 EU/t (3000 K steel, 3600 K tungsten)
- **Watch out:**
    - Tungstic acid is an EV chemical bath recipe (960 EU/t), not a reactor one.
    - One tungsten costs 7 tungstate dust: that ratio paces the era (a motor needs about 17 dust).
    - Tungsten steel: EV mixer 1 tungsten + 1 steel dust, EBF 3000 K 50 s per ingot, then the Vacuum Freezer.
    - Tungsten dust goes into the mixer OR the 3600 K furnace, never both: two drawers.
    - The Alloy Blast Smelter is an EV craft (EV alloy smelter + 2 EV circuits + 4 tantalum carbide plates): do not wait for IV. The CAT (super\_abs) is ZPM.

<a id="netherstar"></a>

## Nether star loop and elemental powders

- **Module key:** `plan:netherstar` · step 56
- **Chain:** [Nether star loop: shards → energized shards → infused shards → impure star → implosion](../chains/iv/nether_star.md); also [Elemental powders: blizz, blitz, basalz from dust + gas, then molten powder for the nether star loop](../chains/iv/elemental.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:platinum_dust`, `gtceu:rhodium_dust`, `gtceu:helium_bucket`
- **Makes:** `minecraft:nether_star`, `kubejs:nether_star_shard`, `gtceu:blizz_bucket`
- **Power:** hammer 240 EU/t; polarizer 960 EU/t; IV autoclave 3840 EU/t; powders 7680 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - 1 nether star -&gt; 5 shards (hammer); the shard polarizer is EV (960 EU/t); the shard autoclave is IV (3840 EU/t).
    - Each powder recipe: 1 dust + 1000 mB gas = 16 powder in 10 s at 7680 EU/t.
    - Molten powder: an LV extractor is enough.
    - Blaze powder has no recipe in the pack (the vanilla one is removed).

## IV circuit and IV hatches

- **Module key:** `plan:ivcirc` · step 57
- **Chain:** [IV circuit: Microprocessor Mainframe (microprocessor → assembly → supercomputer → mainframe, silicon wafers only)](../chains/iv/iv_circuits.md); also [IV Energy and Dynamo Hatch: phosphorus wafer → MPIC wafer → HPIC wafer → 2 HPIC chips, IV voltage coil, IV Assembler](../chains/iv/iv_hatches.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:plastic_printed_circuit_board`, `gtceu:cleanroom`, `gtceu:smd_resistor`, `gtceu:smd_capacitor`, `gtceu:smd_transistor`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:micro_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:iv_energy_input_hatch`, `gtceu:iv_energy_output_hatch`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:micro_processor_mainframe` in 16
- **Power:** IV assembler 7680 EU/t for the hatches
- **Watch out:**
    - First IV circuit = Microprocessor Mainframe: silicon wafers only, no laser lens beyond the HV chain.
    - One phosphorus boule (EBF 2484 K, 10 min) feeds 32 hatches; one HPIC wafer = 2 chips = one hatch.
    - The slow, rare input is molten vanadium gallium (EBF 4500 K).
    - The IV Parallel Hatch needs 4 LuV circuits; the Auto Maintenance Hatch is an HV craft (4 HV circuits, 2 maintenance hatches, 2 HV robot arms, HV hull).

## IV power: thorium Nuclear Reactor (or more turbines)

- **Module key:** `plan:ivpower` · step 58
- **Chain:** [IV power: thorium fuel rods in the Nuclear Reactor, or fuel in the Extreme Combustion Engine, into an IV Dynamo Hatch](../chains/power/power_iv.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:micro_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:iv_energy_output_hatch`, `gtceu:distilled_water_bucket`, `gtceu:nitrobenzene_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:nuclear_reactor`, `kubejs:thr_fuel_rod`
- **Stock:** `kubejs:thr_fuel_rod` in 4
- **Power:** reactor 4 096 EU/t out per rod; canner 1 920 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Reactor controller: 2 IV circuits, an EV robot arm, 2 EV fluid regulators, an EV emitter, a Large Chemical Reactor and 2 heat vents. Rod: canner 600 ticks at 1 920 EU/t; reactor 588 ticks, 4 096 EU/t, 5 B distilled water per rod.
    - Fuel per mB: high octane gasoline 3 200 EU/mB, gasoline 1 600 (combustion engines); nitrobenzene 1 280 (gas turbines only). Large Gas Turbine is EV, the Large Combustion Engine needs 1 IV circuit, the Extreme Combustion Engine 1 LuV circuit.
    - Community reports: the reported setups stay on nitrobenzene turbines in IV and none used the thorium reactor; scaling the EV turbine line with 16A hatches is the common choice.

## IV components, RTM coil, Shellite

- **Module key:** `plan:ivmach` · step 59
- **Chain:** [IV components: tungsten steel parts, motor, piston, pump, conveyor, robot arm, emitter, sensor and hull on LV assemblers](../chains/iv/iv_components.md); also [RTM coil: ruthenium + tungsten + molybdenum → RTM alloy → double wire + vanadium steel foil + borosilicate foil + molten nichrome](../chains/iv/rtm_coil.md), [Shellite: signalum + black bronze → EV Mixer → EBF 4400 K (RTM coils, IV hatch) → wire → IV converters](../chains/iv/shellite_line.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:tungsten_steel_ingot`, `gtceu:micro_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:polytetrafluoroethylene_bucket`, `gtceu:ruthenium_dust`, `gtceu:helium_bucket`, `gtceu:nichrome_ingot`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:iv_electric_motor`, `gtceu:iv_robot_arm`, `gtceu:iv_emitter`, `gtceu:iv_sensor`, `gtceu:iv_machine_hull`, `gtceu:rtm_alloy_coil_block`, `gtceu:shellite_ingot`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:iv_machine_hull` in 8
- **Power:** LV assembler 30 EU/t for the components; EV assembler 1920 EU/t for the RTM coil
- **Watch out:**

    - New problem: IV cables need PVC foil + 72 mB silicone rubber (or styrene butadiene).
    - RTM coil (4500 K): 8 RTM double wire + 8 vanadium steel foil + 8 borosilicate foil + 144 mB molten nichrome.
    - Shellite blasts at 4400 K: RTM coils and an IV energy hatch (7680 EU/t).
