# UV: Abydos first, then wetware

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p14` · **Era:** [UV · Abydos](../eras/uv.md)

PEEK and UV solder, the UV hull, Abydos (xenotime → dysprosium → UV motors → drilling rig and magma), zirconium, then the sterile bio lines, research data and the wetware circuits. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 3 days from the Stargate to UV (1 clean run) and about 3 days in UV (3 runs, 0.7 to 3).

## PEEK, polyimide and UV solder

- **Module key:** `plan:uvchem` · step 75
- **Chain:** [Polyether ether ketone (PEEK): 8 Large Chemical Reactor steps from toluene, benzene, fluorine and propene - the UV hull plastic](../chains/uv/uv_peek.md); also [Polyimide: 12 reactions (maleic anhydride, BTDA, NMP, phenylenediamine, polyamic acid) - needs UV-tier Large Chemical Reactors for the last two steps](../chains/uv/uv_polyimide.md), [UV solder: indium tin lead cadmium soldering alloy in the Alloy Blast Smelter (2880 mB per 14 dust batch, circuit 14, 3000 K)](../chains/uv/uv_solder.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:benzene_bucket`, `gtceu:chlorine_bucket`, `gtceu:oxygen_bucket`, `gtceu:propene_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:polyether_ether_ketone_bucket`, `gtceu:indium_tin_lead_cadmium_soldering_alloy_bucket`
- **Watch out:**
    - PEEK is the UV and UHV hull plastic: 8 Large Chemical Reactor steps.
    - Polyimide: 12 reactions; the last two steps need UV-tier Large Chemical Reactors.
    - UV solder: Alloy Blast Smelter, circuit 14, 14 indium + 3 tin + 2 lead + 1 cadmium = 2880 mB.
    - **Unverified** indium and cadmium sources.

## Abydos first: xenotime → dysprosium → drilling rig and magma

- **Module key:** `plan:abydos` · step 77
- **Chain:** [Xenotime rare earth line: 5 machines → yttrium + small lutetium, dysprosium, europium, samarium dust; dysprosium and zapolgium rods are polarized in the pack’s own recipe](../chains/uv/uv_rare_earth.md); also [Abydos dense magma → residues, lava and 12 raw ores (titanite, xenotime, monazite, scheelite; zapolite, crookesite, kitkaite, lautarite; naquadite, magnesite, chromite, magnetite)](../chains/uv/uv_magma.md), [Abydos mantle: ZPM Fluid Drilling Rig (144 CWU/t research) → gritty akreyrium → Cyclonic Sifter and Molten Destabiliser (280 B and 72 B of it per controller)](../chains/uv/uv_akreyrium.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:uv_machine_hull`, `gtceu:zpm_machine_hull`, `gtceu:crystal_processor_mainframe`, `gtceu:sulfuric_acid_bucket`, `gtceu:polybenzimidazole_bucket`, `gtceu:indium_tin_lead_cadmium_soldering_alloy_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:cyclonic_sifter`, `gtceu:molten_destabiliser`, `gtceu:magnetic_dysprosium_rod`, `gtceu:uv_electric_motor`, `gtceu:uv_electric_pump`
- **Power:** Cyclonic Sifter 98 304 EU/t; Molten Destabiliser 491 520 EU/t (UV hatch)
- **Watch out:**
    - The ZPM Fluid Drilling Rig (4 UV motors + 4 UV pumps, 144 CWU/t research) only drills in Abydos: build the rig and its power line there.
    - Dense magma gives 12 raw ores (titanite, xenotime, monazite, scheelite, zapolite, naquadite...): sift first, about 7 times more ore per magma.
    - The xenotime rare earth line gives only 0.1 dust of each rare earth per run: build many.
    - Not a loop: dysprosium comes from Abydos ORE (xenotime in the refractory metals vein, mined by hand), not from magma: xenotime → rare earth line (5 steps, EBF 7 500 K = trinium coils) → dysprosium → long magnetic dysprosium rod → the first UV motors and pumps → ZPM Fluid Drilling Rig → gritty akreyrium (72 000 mB) → Molten Destabiliser → magma.
    - Fluid Drilling Rig: UV hull + 4 UV circuits (crystal mainframe or wetware computer) + 4 UV motors + 4 UV pumps + 4 naquadah alloy gears + 1 872 mB solder, research 144 CWU/t. Molten Destabiliser: ZPM hull + 6 UV circuits + 24 ZPM pumps + 4 pure netherite rotors + 64 UHPIC chips, PBI 8 000 + gritty akreyrium 72 000 mB. Both run at UV voltage.
    - The akreyrium steps need a UHV hatch (up to 2 097 152 EU/t) and one UIV step: only the drilling for gritty akreyrium belongs here, the rest waits for UHV.
    - Zapolgium can start here: EBF 10 299 K (tritanium coil, assembled at UV voltage) with zapolite from Abydos and tritanium from MK II; it does not wait for the UV fusion reactor (module uhvmetals).
