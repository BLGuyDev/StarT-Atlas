<a id="coils"></a>

## Cupronickel coils

- **Module key:** `plan:coils` · step 28
- **Chain:** [Cupronickel coils ×16](../chains/lv/coils.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:cupronickel_ingot`, `gtceu:bronze_ingot`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:cupronickel_coil_block`
- **Watch out:**
    - 16 coils = 128 double wires + 128 foils + 2304 mB tin alloy.
    - The Electric Blast Furnace (aluminium, steel with oxygen, wafers) needs them.

<a id="aluminium"></a>

## Aluminium ingots

- **Module key:** `plan:aluminium` · step 29
- **Chain:** [Infinite aluminium (clay)](../chains/mv/aluminium.md)
- **Makes:** `gtceu:aluminium_dust`, `gtceu:aluminium_ingot`, `gtceu:silicon_dust`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:aluminium_ingot` in 64
- **Watch out:**
    - Pack recipe: 11 sodalite dust → 3 aluminium dust + 3 silicon dust in an electrolyzer (30 EU/t, 26 s). Sodalite comes from the Void Extractor.
    - Aluminium has blast temperature 1700 K: dust → ingot only in the EBF (120 EU/t); cupronickel coils are enough.
    - The clay route in the chain needs the MV Electrolyzer, so on LV power use the sodalite route first.
