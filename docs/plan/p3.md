# Base materials

<a id="treated"></a>

## Treated wood

- **Module key:** `plan:treated` · step 18
- **Chain:** [Treated wood: cutter + spout](../chains/lv/treated_wood.md)
- **Uses:** `minecraft:oak_log`, `gtceu:creosote_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:treated_wood_planks`, `gtceu:wood_dust`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:treated_wood_planks` in drawer
- **Watch out:**
    - The cutter needs water (lubricant doubles speed).
    - 125 mB creosote per plank.

<a id="rubber"></a>

## Rubber and liquid rubber

- **Module key:** `plan:rubber` · step 19
- **Chain:** [Rubber: Large Arboreal Extractor](../chains/steam/rubber.md); also [Fertilizer (for the rubber extractor and greenhouses)](../chains/lv/fertilizer.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:wood_dust`, `minecraft:sand`, `gtceu:sulfur_dust`
- **Makes:** `thermal:rubber`, `gtceu:rubber_bucket`, `gtceu:rubber_plate`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:rubber_bucket` in 16 B, `gtceu:rubber_plate` in 64
- **Watch out:**
    - Large Arboreal Extractor around a jungle tree you place; iron screw + fertilizer = 250 mB / 20 s.
    - Chemical reactor: 3 rubber + sulfur = 576 mB liquid rubber (cables, conveyors).

## Glue

- **Module key:** `plan:glue` · step 20

## Steel

- **Module key:** `plan:steel` · step 21
- **Chain:** [Steel: Solid Blast Furnace](../chains/steam/steel.md); also [Steel: Primitive Blast Furnace](../chains/steam/steel_pbf.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:wrought_iron_ingot`, `minecraft:charcoal`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:steel_ingot`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:steel_ingot` in drawer

## Alloys (bronze, red alloy, cupronickel, tin alloy)

- **Module key:** `plan:alloys` · step 22
- **Chain:** [Alloys: Steam / LV Alloy Smelter (more per ingot)](../chains/steam/alloys_smelter.md); also [Alloys: Create heated mixer](../chains/steam/alloys.md)
