# Stone age

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p0` · **Era:** [Stone Age](../eras/stone.md)

Cobblestone, hand sieving, clay and bricks: the first ores and the furnace bricks before any machine. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 2.5 days from the start to MV in total (median of 4 runs).

<a id="cobble"></a>

## Cobblestone and blackstone

- **Module key:** `plan:cobble` · step 1
- **Chain:** [Infinite cobble &amp; blackstone](../chains/stone/inf_cobble.md)
- **Makes:** `minecraft:cobblestone`, `minecraft:blackstone`
- **Watch out:**
    - Stone barrel with circuit 10: lava in plus water inside = obsidian.
    - Witch water instead of water gives blackstone.
    - The Large Stone Barrel does all of this much faster later.

<a id="handsieve"></a>

## Hand sieve

- **Module key:** `plan:handsieve` · step 2
- **Chain:** [Sieve station (manual)](../chains/stone/sieve_manual.md)
- **Uses:** `minecraft:cobblestone`
- **Makes:** `minecraft:gravel`, `gtceu:crushed_copper_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_iron_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_tin_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_sphalerite_ore`, `gtceu:crushed_magnetite_ore`
- **Watch out:**
    - Hammer cobble to gravel, gravel to sand, sand to dust; sieve each for different drops (tables in Resources).
    - Mud gives slitake and mycelium spores; dirt gives every sapling.

<a id="barrels"></a>

## Barrel transformations

- **Module key:** `plan:barrels` · step 3
- **Chain:** [Barrel transformations](../chains/stone/barrel.md)
- **Makes:** `minecraft:clay`, `exnihilosequentia:witch_water_bucket`, `minecraft:soul_sand`
- **Watch out:**
    - Dust plus water = clay; mycelium spores plus water = witch water; witch water plus sand = soul sand.
    - Cobble plus witch water = blackstone.

## Firebricks and coke oven bricks

- **Module key:** `plan:bricks` · step 4
