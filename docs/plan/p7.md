# HV: stainless, Kanthal and air

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p7` · **Era:** [HV](../eras/hv.md)

Chromium from the Void Extractor, stainless steel and Kanthal coils, and the air gases that speed up every blast furnace. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 5 days in HV (median of 5 runs, range 1 to 16).

<a id="chromium"></a>

## Chromium (Void Extractor residue)

- **Module key:** `plan:chromium` · step 39
- **Chain:** [Chromium: Void Extractor residue → chromite dust → electrolyzer → chromium dust, ingot and rod](../chains/hv/chromium_line.md); also [Rare Ore Residue](../chains/mv/rare_residue.md)
- **Makes:** `gtceu:chromium_dust`, `gtceu:chromium_rod`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:chromium_dust` in drum
- **Power:** Void Extractor 30 EU/t; residue electrolyzer 30 EU/t; MV electrolyzer 60 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Stainless, Kanthal and nichrome all need chromium dust, so this line comes first.
    - 1 chromium dust = 3.5 chromite dust = about 4.4 extractor runs; build two or three extractors early.
    - The residue electrolyzer gives four sludge dusts at once: give each its own drawer or the output bus clogs.
    - **Unverified** chromite electrolysis (7 dust -&gt; 1 iron + 2 chromium + 4 B oxygen) is derived from the code, not read from EMI.
    - This line can start at the end of MV: Void Extractor (LV) + LV electrolyzer + MV centrifuge + MV electrolyzer. Demand check: 15 stacks of stainless = about 107 chromium + 107 manganese dust.

<a id="air"></a>

## Air: oxygen and nitrogen

- **Module key:** `plan:air` · step 40
- **Chain:** [Oxygen &amp; nitrogen from air (Gas Collector + Centrifuge)](../chains/hv/oxygen.md); also [Steel: Electric Blast Furnace + oxygen](../chains/mv/steel_ebf.md)
- **Makes:** `gtceu:oxygen_bucket`, `gtceu:nitrogen_bucket`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:nitrogen_bucket` in drum
- **Power:** MV gas collector 64 EU/t; MV centrifuge 112 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - One collector (120 B of air a minute) feeds four MV centrifuges: add centrifuges, not collectors.
    - 10 B of air gives 3.9 B nitrogen + 1 B oxygen.
    - Nitrogen is the real consumer: 1 B per ingot speeds up the stainless and Kanthal blast furnaces.

<a id="stainless"></a>

## Stainless steel and Kanthal

- **Module key:** `plan:stainless` · step 41
- **Chain:** [Stainless steel: MV Mixer + HV Electric Blast Furnace](../chains/hv/stainless.md); also [Kanthal: MV Mixer, HV EBF, chemical bath, wire, spring and coil block](../chains/hv/kanthal.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:chromium_dust`, `gtceu:nitrogen_bucket`, `gtceu:aluminium_ingot`, `minecraft:copper_ingot`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:stainless_steel_ingot`, `gtceu:kanthal_coil_block`, `gtceu:kanthal_spring`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:stainless_steel_ingot` in 64
- **Power:** HV EBF 480 EU/t (stainless 1700 K, Kanthal 1800 K); MV mixer 120 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - The EBF recipes are 480 EU/t: build the HV Energy Hatch, an MV hatch refuses them.
    - Mixer circuit 3: 6 iron + 1 nickel + 1 manganese + 1 chromium dust = 9 stainless dust. Kanthal on circuit 1: 1 iron + 1 aluminium + 1 chromium.
    - The hot Kanthal ingot needs a chemical bath (100 mB water); the coil block is 8 double wire + 8 aluminium foil + 144 mB copper.
    - Chromium is the bottleneck: 16 Kanthal coils eat 128 chromium dust.
    - Stainless (EBF 1700 K) and Kanthal (EBF 1800 K) are 480 EU/t recipes: an HV hatch, or two 2 A MV hatches on one EBF (4 A at MV = 1 A at HV), so both can be prepared at the end of MV.
