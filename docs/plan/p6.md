# MV circuits and chemistry

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p6` · **Era:** [MV](../eras/mv.md)

The first MV circuit: acid and etchant from water, silver foil boards, gallium arsenide diodes. All of it runs on LV machines. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 5 days in MV (median of 7 runs, range 1 to 17).

<a id="chlor"></a>

## Chlorine and hydrochloric acid

- **Module key:** `plan:chlor` · step 32
- **Chain:** [Chlorine and hydrochloric acid (water → salt water → acid)](../chains/mv/mv_chlorine.md); also [Salt water](../chains/mv/sea_water.md)
- **Makes:** `gtceu:salt_water_bucket`, `gtceu:chlorine_bucket`, `gtceu:hydrogen_bucket`, `gtceu:hydrochloric_acid_bucket`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:hydrochloric_acid_bucket` in 6 B drum
- **Power:** centrifuge 15 EU/t, electrolyzer 30 EU/t, reactor 7 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Only water and one kept sand block go in; salt water is made on site.
    - The electrolyzer takes 36 s per bucket: pipe chlorine and hydrogen straight into the acid reactor, equal amounts.
    - Sodium hydroxide, saltpeter and rock salt pile up: give them a drawer or a voiding cover.
    - This phase needs no oxygen, sulfuric acid or MV machine; those come later.

<a id="fecl3"></a>

## Etching fluid (iron III chloride)

- **Module key:** `plan:fecl3` · step 33
- **Chain:** [Etching fluid (iron III chloride)](../chains/mv/mv_fecl3.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:hydrochloric_acid_bucket`, `minecraft:iron_ingot`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:iron_iii_chloride_bucket`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:iron_iii_chloride_bucket` in 4 B drum
- **Power:** reactor 30 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Set ghost circuit 1 on the reactor.
    - Each bucket also gives 3 B of hydrogen: put a Fluid Voiding Cover (soft mallet) or a drum on that output or the reactor stalls.
    - 100 mB etches one board; 4 B covers 40 boards.

<a id="pcb"></a>

## Phenolic printed circuit boards

- **Module key:** `plan:pcb` · step 34
- **Chain:** [Phenolic printed circuit board (silver foil + etching)](../chains/mv/mv_board.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:glue_bucket`, `gtceu:wood_dust`, `gtceu:iron_iii_chloride_bucket`, `gtceu:crushed_galena_ore`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:silver_foil`, `gtceu:phenolic_circuit_board`, `gtceu:phenolic_printed_circuit_board`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:silver_foil` in 64, `gtceu:phenolic_printed_circuit_board` in 64
- **Power:** ore factory, bender, assembler, reactor: 30 EU/t each
- **Watch out:**
    - 1 board = 1 silver ingot (4 foil): silver is the bottleneck of the phase.
    - Bender on circuit 10 for ingots (circuit 1 is for plates).
    - The hand-crafted good circuit board is removed in this pack: etching is the only way.
    - Ignore the pack’s cupric chloride etch until you have HV and EV machines.

<a id="gaas"></a>

## Gallium arsenide

- **Module key:** `plan:gaas` · step 35
- **Chain:** [Gallium arsenide (sphalerite + realgar)](../chains/mv/mv_gaas.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:crushed_sphalerite_ore`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:gallium_dust`, `gtceu:arsenic_dust`, `gtceu:gallium_arsenide_dust`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:gallium_arsenide_dust` in 16
- **Power:** ore factory, void extractor, pulverizer, centrifuge 30 EU/t; mixer 7 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Gallium only from sphalerite in the Electric Ore Factory (45 %); plain electrolysis gives 5 %.
    - Arsenic only from realgar, and realgar only from the Void Extractor: start this line early, it is the slow one.
    - Equal gallium and arsenic: 1 + 1 = 4 MV circuits. 1 arsenic needs 2 realgar dust = 1 raw realgar.
    - Unverified in local files: the centrifuge numbers for realgar (1 arsenic per 2 dust, about 64 s); check EMI.
    - Silicon wafers need the same gallium arsenide later (1 small dust per boule).

<a id="diodes"></a>

## Diodes

- **Module key:** `plan:diodes` · step 36
- **Chain:** [Diodes (gallium arsenide + glass + fine wire)](../chains/mv/mv_diode.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:gallium_arsenide_dust`, `gtceu:fine_copper_wire`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:small_gallium_arsenide_dust`, `gtceu:diode`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:diode` in 64
- **Power:** assemblers 30 EU/t each
- **Watch out:**
    - Small dust: 1 gallium arsenide dust → 4 small dust (grid, or Packer circuit 2).
    - Glass must be molten: glass block → LV Extractor → 144 mB (per diode). Not confirmed in local files: check the extractor recipe in EMI.
    - One assembler makes 3 diodes a minute; build three per circuit assembler.
    - Annealed copper wire makes 2 diodes per craft instead of 1.

<a id="wafer"></a>

## Silicon wafers and chips

- **Module key:** `plan:wafer` · step 37
- **Chain:** [Silicon wafers &amp; chips](../chains/mv/wafer.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:silicon_dust`, `gtceu:gallium_arsenide_dust`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:silicon_wafer`, `gtceu:ilc_chip`, `gtceu:ram_chip`, `gtceu:ulpic_chip`, `gtceu:cpu_chip`
- **Power:** EBF with cupronickel coils, MV cutter and MV laser engraver
- **Watch out:**
    - Engraved wafer to cutter to chips.
    - One engraver per lens avoids swapping lenses.
    - CPU, RAM and NAND chips go into the data sticks and the LuV boards.

<a id="mvcirc"></a>

## MV circuits (Good Electronic Circuit)

- **Module key:** `plan:mvcirc` · step 38
- **Chain:** [MV circuit line (Good Electronic Circuit)](../chains/mv/mv_circuit.md); also [MV Robot Arm (uses the new circuits)](../chains/mv/comp_mv_arm.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:basic_electronic_circuit`, `gtceu:phenolic_printed_circuit_board`, `gtceu:diode`, `gtceu:copper_single_wire`, `gtceu:mv_electric_motor`, `gtceu:mv_electric_piston`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:good_electronic_circuit`, `gtceu:mv_robot_arm`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:good_electronic_circuit` in 64, `gtceu:mv_robot_arm` in 16
- **Power:** circuit assembler 30 EU/t (LV machine, 4 circuits a minute)
- **Watch out:**
    - Recipe is 30 EU/t: the LV circuit assembler runs it. Use a second one so your LV circuits keep their own machine.
    - The MV circuit assembler needs 2 HV circuits to build, so it comes much later.
    - Each circuit eats 2 LV circuits: keep the LV line at 64 in stock first.
    - Integrated circuits are the next step, not needed for MV machines: silicon boule (EBF 1784 K, 120 EU/t, 7.5 min, 32 silicon dust + 1 small gallium arsenide dust) → MV cutter → MV laser engraver with a red lens → MV cutter → ILC chips.
