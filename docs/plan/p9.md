# EV: titanium, platinum and nitrobenzene power

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p9` · **Era:** [EV](../eras/ev.md)

Titanium first (EV hull), refinery gases and radon, nichrome coils, the platinum group, the EV component set and nitrobenzene gas turbines. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 12 days in EV (median of 4 runs, range 1 to 18).

<a id="titanium"></a>

## Titanium (molten ore line)

- **Module key:** `plan:titanium` · step 49
- **Chain:** [Titanium: molten ilmenite → rutile → TiCl4 → Kanthal EBF → Vacuum Freezer](../chains/ev/titanium_line.md); also [Molten Ore Mixture](../chains/ev/molten_mix.md), [Raw Ore Slurry &amp; mineral mixtures](../chains/mv/slurry.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:chlorine_bucket`, `gtceu:kanthal_coil_block`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:titanium_ingot`, `gtceu:molten_ore_mixture_bucket`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:titanium_ingot` in 64
- **Power:** EBF 480 EU/t at 2141 K (Kanthal coils, HV hatch); EV mixer 1920 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Molten ore mixture is the bottleneck: EV mixer, 16 crushed ores + 32 stone dust + 32 B lava = 6 B in 40 s.
    - Titanium needs 2141 K: cupronickel fails, Kanthal coils and an HV hatch work.
    - Per ingot: 1 rutile, 2 carbon, 4 B chlorine and 2 magnesium; magnesium and chlorine come back from the MgCl2 electrolysis.
    - A full set of EV single machines eats dozens of ingots.
    - Titanium first: the EV hull is 8 titanium plates + 2 aluminium cables + 288 mB polyethylene. Before the molten ore line runs (Distillation Tower and mixer are EV), the quest shop sells 16 titanium for 16 coins, or use titanium dust from the ruby slurry.
    - Molten ore voltages: Void Extractor LV, slurry centrifuge MV, raw ore autoclave HV (500 mB + stone dust, 160 ticks), recycling mixer and distillation tower EV (1 920 EU/t), looping mixer IV.

<a id="evfluids"></a>

## Refinery gases, liquid air, radon, epoxy

- **Module key:** `plan:evfluids` · step 50
- **Chain:** [Oil refinery](../chains/ev/refinery.md); also [Liquid air distillation](../chains/ev/air.md), [Radon](../chains/ev/radon.md), [Epoxy](../chains/ev/epoxy.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:oil_heavy_bucket`, `gtceu:hydrogen_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:ethylene_bucket`, `gtceu:propene_bucket`, `gtceu:helium_bucket`, `gtceu:radon_bucket`, `gtceu:epoxy_bucket`
- **Watch out:**
    - Lock each refinery output hatch to its fluid.
    - Nether air: 3 netherrack dust + 32 B air (HV mixer); ender air: 3 end stone dust + 32 B nether air.
    - LCR circuit 24 skips most epoxy steps.
    - Radon feeds quantum eyes and stars and the EV circuit assembler; epoxy is the LuV board plastic.
    - Distillation Tower = HV hull + 4 EV circuits; Vacuum Freezer = 3 EV circuits: this line waits for the first EV circuits (cleanroom, previous phase).
    - Radon in EV only by the LCR plutonium route: 8 plutonium-239 ingots (returned) + 1 uranium-238 dust + 10 B air → 1 B radon, 480 EU/t, 4 000 ticks. The liquid ender air route (vacuum freezer + tower) is IV (7 680 EU/t). The EV emitter and sensor need it (quantum eye = ender eye + 250 mB radon), so plan it before the EV components.

## Platinum group line

- **Module key:** `plan:platinum` · step 52
- **Chain:** [Platinum group line: sludge → aqua regia centrifuge → platinum, palladium, rhodium, ruthenium, iridium, osmium](../chains/iv/platline.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:hydrochloric_acid_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:platinum_dust`, `gtceu:palladium_dust`, `gtceu:rhodium_dust`, `gtceu:ruthenium_dust`, `gtceu:iridium_dust`, `gtceu:osmium_dust`
- **Power:** LV reactor, mixer 30 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Sludge: LV chemical reactor, 1 purified ore + 100 mB nitric acid (50 ticks).
    - Aqua regia: LV mixer, 1 B nitric + 2 B hydrochloric acid = 3 B; the centrifuge takes 1200 mB per 6 sludge.
    - Rhodium is the rare input of the nether star loop.
    - Moved to EV: PGS separation is an HV centrifuge (480 EU/t), platinum and palladium need only MV, rhodium and ruthenium an EV chemical reactor (1 920 EU/t). Iridium and osmium need an IV Large Chemical Reactor (7 680 EU/t): run that last step in IV.
    - Platinum is needed already in EV: EV sensor and emitter (platinum rods), EV transformers and the 4A/16A EV hatches (platinum cable), SMD diodes (fine platinum wire); ruthenium feeds the Assembler Matrix and the RTM coils.

## EV power: nitrobenzene gas turbines

- **Module key:** `plan:evpower` · step 54
- **Chain:** [EV power: nitrobenzene in Large Gas Turbines (1 280 EU per mB)](../chains/ev/ev_nitrobenzene.md); also [EV power](../chains/power/power_ev.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:benzene_bucket`, `gtceu:sulfuric_acid_bucket`, `gtceu:distilled_water_bucket`, `gtceu:ev_machine_hull`, `gtceu:micro_processor_computer`, `gtceu:stainless_steel_ingot`

    - Large Gas Turbine = EV hull + 2 EV circuits + 4 stainless gears + 2 stainless large fluid pipes; one rotor holder per turbine, never shared.
