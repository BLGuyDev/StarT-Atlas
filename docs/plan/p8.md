# HV: plastics and circuits

<!-- Generated from the front matter by tools/refresh_body.js. Edit the front matter, not this text. -->

**Phase id:** `p8` · **Era:** [HV](../eras/hv.md)

Oil without wells, polyethylene and PTFE, a cleanroom, SMDs, the first HV and EV circuits, the HV component set and diatron lapidary power. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 5 days in HV (median of 5 runs, range 1 to 16).

<a id="oil"></a>

## Oil without wells (pyrolyse + distillery)

- **Module key:** `plan:oil` · step 42
- **Chain:** [Oil without oil: Pyrolyse Oven on logs, wood dust, plant balls or bio chaff](../chains/hv/oil_pyro.md); also [Oil in a Distillery (no tower): methane from light oil, benzene from heavy oil](../chains/hv/oil_distill.md), [Methane &amp; wood chemicals](../chains/mv/wood_gas.md), [Sulfuric acid](../chains/mv/sulfuric.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:wood_dust`, `gtceu:hydrogen_bucket`, `gtceu:oxygen_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:oil_heavy_bucket`, `gtceu:oil_light_bucket`, `gtceu:benzene_bucket`, `gtceu:methane_bucket`, `gtceu:sulfuric_acid_bucket`
- **Power:** pyrolyse oven 96 EU/t (heavy oil 384 EU/t); distillery 24 to 72 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - Heavy oil from logs is the only 384 EU/t recipe: it needs an HV Energy Hatch.
    - Every tower recipe also exists in the single-block Distillery: one recipe per output, the circuit is the output position.
    - Hydrogen sulfide is not waste: 1 B + 4 B oxygen = 1 B sulfuric acid.
    - **Unverified** whether an LV or MV reactor tank holds the 16 B and 8 B desulfurizing batches (read the tooltip).

<a id="plastics"></a>

## Polyethylene and PTFE

- **Module key:** `plan:plastics` · step 43
- **Chain:** [Polyethylene](../chains/mv/polyethylene.md); also [PTFE: three HV Chemical Reactors](../chains/hv/ptfe.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:sulfuric_acid_bucket`, `gtceu:oxygen_bucket`, `gtceu:methane_bucket`, `gtceu:chlorine_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:polyethylene_bucket`, `gtceu:plastic_printed_circuit_board`, `gtceu:polytetrafluoroethylene_bucket`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:polyethylene_bucket` in 8 B, `gtceu:plastic_printed_circuit_board` in 64
- **Watch out:**
    - Oxygen gives 1.5x the polymer of air.
    - PTFE: chlorine is the bottleneck, one LV electrolyzer makes 1.7 B a minute and an HV one about 6.7.
    - **Unverified** fluorine has no recipe in the pack scripts (it must come from topaz, lepidolite or bastnasite): check EMI before planning PTFE.
    - Diluted sulfuric acid comes back: re-concentrate it.

<a id="cleanroom"></a>

## Cleanroom (5x5x5)

- **Module key:** `plan:cleanroom` · step 44
- **Chain:** [Cleanroom (5×5×5): plascrete, filter casings, one energy hatch](../chains/hv/hv_cleanroom.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:polyethylene_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:cleanroom`, `gtceu:cleanroom_glass`
- **Power:** 30 / 120 / 480 EU/t (LV / MV / HV hatch) while cleaning
- **Watch out:**
    - Not needed for the first HV circuits; it matters for phosphorus wafers, SoCs and every EV circuit.
    - Smallest size: 89 plascrete / glass blocks, 8 filter casings in the ceiling, 1 energy hatch, 1 maintenance hatch.
    - About 45 steel frames + 270 polyethylene plates (about 39 B) + 6.5 B concrete: plan the plastic first.
    - No generators, mufflers or other cleanrooms inside: keep the EBFs outside.

## SMDs and HV circuits

- **Module key:** `plan:hvcirc` · step 45
- **Chain:** [HV circuits: Advanced Integrated Circuit and Microprocessor Assembly](../chains/hv/circuits_hv.md); also [SMDs from HV Assemblers (one machine per part)](../chains/hv/smd.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:plastic_printed_circuit_board`, `gtceu:polyethylene_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:smd_resistor`, `gtceu:smd_transistor`, `gtceu:smd_capacitor`, `gtceu:advanced_integrated_circuit`, `gtceu:micro_processor_assembly`, `gtceu:micro_processor_computer`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:advanced_integrated_circuit` in 32
- **Power:** HV assembler 480 EU/t for every SMD
- **Watch out:**
    - First HV circuit = Advanced Integrated Circuit: no cleanroom, an LV Circuit Assembler runs it.
    - Microprocessor Assembly needs the MV Circuit Assembler.
    - Stock 32 of one type first: HV assembler, reactor and mixer take 2 each, the LCR 4.
    - **Unverified** sources of gallium foil, silicone rubber foil and nickel zinc ferrite were not traced.
    - First EV circuit = Microprocessor Supercomputer: a 120 EU/t cleanroom recipe, so an MV Circuit Assembler inside the cleanroom makes it; the HV Circuit Assembler (which itself needs EV circuits) only speeds it up.

## HV components (on LV assemblers)

- **Module key:** `plan:hvcomp` · step 46
- **Chain:** [HV components 1/2: motor, pump, conveyor on LV assemblers](../chains/hv/hv_components.md); also [HV components 2/2: piston, robot arm, emitter, sensor (and field generator) on LV assemblers](../chains/hv/hv_components2.md)

- **Makes:** `gtceu:hv_electric_motor`, `gtceu:hv_electric_piston`, `gtceu:hv_electric_pump`, `gtceu:hv_conveyor_module`, `gtceu:hv_robot_arm`, `gtceu:hv_emitter`, `gtceu:hv_sensor`
- **Power:** LV assembler 30 EU/t, 5 s per part
- **Watch out:**
    - Every HV component recipe is a 5 s, 30 EU/t assembler recipe: an LV assembler runs them all.
    - Motors first and biggest (12 a minute per assembler): piston 1, pump 1, conveyor 2, robot arm 3.
    - One HV Assembler needs 2 arms + 2 conveyors = 10 motors, plus 4 HV circuits.
    - Emitter and sensor go into the HV Laser Engraver (2 pistons + 1 emitter).

<a id="hvpower"></a>

## HV power: diatron gems in lapidary dynamos

- **Module key:** `plan:hvpower` · step 48
- **Chain:** [HV power: diatron gems in Lapidary Dynamos](../chains/power/power_hv.md)
- **Uses:** `thermal:dynamo_lapidary`, `minecraft:diamond`, `gtceu:hv_machine_hull`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:diatron_gem`, `gtceu:hv_4a_energy_converter`
- **Stock:** `gtceu:diatron_gem` in 64
- **Power:** about 750 EU/t per lapidary dynamo with an HV kit (pack code value)
- **Watch out:**
    - HV Upgrade Kit: the pack code gives x15 (3 000 RF/t = 750 EU/t per dynamo), the quests say x24 and community reports measured about 4 800 RF/t: read the dynamo tooltip. At x15, 24 dynamos are 18 kEU/t, not the 30 kEU/t quoted in community reports (that needs ARC kits).
    - Machine Capacity (MCI) kits raise energy per fuel, not EU/t: 1.45x per HV kit, 1.6x per EV kit, multiplicative (3 HV MCI = about 3.05x = 914 700 RF per diamond).
    - Flux Networks is an HV option after all: the quest shop sells 8 flux plugs + 8 flux points for 16 coins once you make the Large Chemical Reactor (flux dust itself is an EV LCR recipe).
    - No Mystical Agriculture in this pack: diamonds come from the sand sieve (7.5 %) or the Rock Filtrator diamond geode.
