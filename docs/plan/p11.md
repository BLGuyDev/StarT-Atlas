# LuV: nano line

**Phase id:** `p11` · **Era:** [LuV](../eras/luv.md)

Advanced boards and nano chips, the Nanoprocessor Mainframe, the LuV metals, PBI plastic, the Assembly Line parts, naquadah and LuV rotor holders for the turbines. Typical pace (community reports, not independent samples): players report about 7.5 days in LuV (median of 4 runs, range 2 to 17).

## Advanced boards and nano chips

- **Module key:** `plan:luvboards` · step 60
- **Chain:** [Advanced Circuit Board: epoxy plate + gold foil → advanced board → electrum foil + iron III chloride → 6 per Nanoprocessor Mainframe](../chains/luv/luv_boards.md); also [LuV chips: Nano CPU chip (CPU wafer + carbon fibers + molten glowstone) and NOR memory chip (phosphorus wafer)](../chains/luv/luv_chips.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:epoxy_bucket`, `gtceu:sulfuric_acid_bucket`, `gtceu:iron_iii_chloride_bucket`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:epoxy_printed_circuit_board`, `gtceu:nano_cpu_chip`, `gtceu:nor_memory_chip`
- **Power:** chemical reactor 30 EU/t (epoxy board)
- **Watch out:**
    - One Nanoprocessor Mainframe eats 6 advanced boards (48 gold foil, 48 electrum foil).
    - The NOR memory chip has no silicon recipe: it needs a phosphorus-doped wafer in the cleanroom (laser engraver, pink lens).
    - Raw carbon fibers: autoclave, 4 carbon dust + 36 mB polyethylene (an LV autoclave runs it).

## Naquadah and PCB coolant

- **Module key:** `plan:naquadah` · step 65
- **Chain:** [Naquadah: nether star shard + PCB coolant in the Dimensional Destabiliser → raw naquadah → ore line → dust → EBF 5000 K; byproducts by the fluoroantimonic acid line](../chains/luv/naquadah.md); also [PCB coolant: benzene → biphenyl → polychlorinated biphenyl → mix with distilled water (HV machines)](../chains/luv/pcb_coolant.md)

- **Makes:** `gtceu:naquadah_dust`, `gtceu:naquadah_ingot`, `gtceu:pcb_coolant_bucket`
- **Power:** Destabiliser controller: IV assembler 7680 EU/t
- **Watch out:**
    - 1 nether star shard + 8 B PCB coolant = 20 raw naquadah + 9.6 B hot coolant; the Vacuum Freezer returns 1000 mB cold per 1200 mB hot.
