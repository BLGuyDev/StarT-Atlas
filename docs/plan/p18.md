# UEV: nether metals and Hell Forge

## Nether magma: mythril, adamantine, estalt

- **Module key:** `plan:uevnether` · step 92
- **Chain:** [Nether magma line: highly unstable nether magma → mystical magma → mythrillic, adamantamite and estaltadyne dusts (start of mythril, adamantine and estalt)](../chains/uev/uev_nether.md); also [Mythril, adamantine, estalt, calamatium and isovol from the nether dusts (Advanced Synthesis Plant, large chemical reactor, EBF, vacuum chemical reactor)](../chains/uev/uev_nether2.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:manifold_centrifuge`, `gtceu:hydrochloric_acid_bucket`, `gtceu:hydrogen_bucket`, `gtceu:mega_blast_furnace`, `gtceu:mega_vacuum_freezer`
- **Makes:** `gtceu:mythrillic_dust`, `gtceu:isovol_dust`
- **Power:** Manifold Centrifuge 1200 ticks at UHV power
- **Watch out:**
    - Highly unstable nether magma -&gt; mystical magma -&gt; mythrillic, adamantamite and estaltadyne dusts.
    - Mythril, adamantine and estalt each run in the Advanced Synthesis Plant at UEV power.

## Hell Forge, Chiller and UEV alloys

- **Module key:** `plan:uevforge` · step 93
- **Chain:** [UEV multiblock controllers: Heart of the Flame → Hell Forge, Cryostate Quantum Chiller, Mega ABS, Super Compact Heat Chamber (all Assembly Line + station research)](../chains/uev/uev_multis.md); also [Cryostate Quantum Chiller coolants: superstate helium 3 → BEC oganesson (the loop that freezes every UEV and UIV alloy)](../chains/uev/uev_chiller.md), [UEV alloys (mythrolic, magmada, starium): molten metals + element plasma in the Hell Forge → alloy plasma → Cryostate Quantum Chiller → molten alloy → vacuum freezer → ingot](../chains/uev/uev_alloys.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:uhv_field_generator`, `gtceu:uhv_robot_arm`, `gtceu:heat_chamber`, `kubejs:uepic_chip`, `gtceu:mythrillic_dust`, `gtceu:isovol_dust`
- **Makes:** `start_core:hellforge`, `gtceu:cryostate_quantum_chiller`, `gtceu:mythrolic_alloy_ingot`, `gtceu:magmada_alloy_ingot`, `gtceu:starium_alloy_ingot`
- **Watch out:**
    - Every UEV alloy is made as PLASMA in the Hell Forge, cooled in the Cryostate Quantum Chiller, then the vacuum freezer.
    - The Chiller is about 2800 blocks (776 reinforced cryostone casings, 640 void frames...).
    - The coolant loop loses 50 mB oganesson per 144 mB of plasma: top it up.
    - Cryostate Quantum Chiller, two modes: quantum cooling (plasma 144 + BEC oganesson 250 mB → molten + 200 oganesson) and vacuum freezer (molten + 500 mB superstate helium-3 → ingot). The Hell Forge has one recipe logic (heating recipes first); every 450 MK over the requirement gives 2^n absolute parallels.

## UEV fusion and plasma power

- **Module key:** `plan:uevfusion` · step 95
- **Chain:** [UEV fusion parts: borealic reflector panel and casing (tier 5), advanced fusion coil, fusion casing MK4 → UEV Fusion Reactor](../chains/uev/uev_fusion.md); also [UEV power: plasma → Supreme Plasma Turbine with a UEV rotor holder (tungsten disulfide + superstate helium 3 boost) → laser or dynamo hatch](../chains/power/uev_power.md)
- **Uses:** `gtceu:uv_field_generator`, `gtceu:mythrolic_alloy_ingot`, `gtceu:starium_alloy_ingot`, `gtceu:uev_machine_hull`
- **Makes:** `start_core:uev_fusion_reactor`, `gtceu:uev_256a_laser_source_hatch`
- **Watch out:**
    - Borealic reflector panel and casing (tier 5), advanced fusion coil, fusion casing MK4.
    - The UEV fusion reactor needs 4 UIV circuits.
    - Power: plasma through the Supreme Plasma Turbine with a UEV rotor holder.
    - Supreme Plasma Turbine (lang): 16 384 EU/t base, x2 per rotor holder tier over IV (UEV holder = 524 288 EU/t), +10 % efficiency per tier, 6 parallels, lubricant +25 %, superstate helium-3 coolant +75 %. Oganesson plasma = fusion tier 5: 144 fermium + 360 selenium → 1 500 mB, 61 440 EU/t.
    - End access (before UIV): the End crystal comes from a Dimensional Finder in the Nether, 16 UHV sensors, 7 200 mB echo, 384 CWU/t, UIV voltage (31 457 280 EU/t): get UEV 4A, better 16A, hatches first.
