## Wood farm

- **Module key:** `plan:wood` · step 5
- **Chain:** [Create tree farm (rotating saws + deployers)](../chains/steam/tree_farm.md); also [Infinite wood &amp; charcoal](../chains/mv/wood.md)
- **Makes:** `minecraft:oak_log`, `minecraft:oak_sapling`
- **Stock:** `minecraft:oak_log` in 2k drawer
- **Watch out:**
    - Saws on the moving arm fell whole trees.
    - Logs go into one drawer that every later line pulls from.

## Charcoal and creosote

- **Module key:** `plan:fuel` · step 6
- **Chain:** [Charcoal: Create fan smelting](../chains/steam/charcoal_fan.md); also [Charcoal &amp; creosote: Coke Oven](../chains/steam/charcoal.md)
- **Uses:** `minecraft:oak_log`
- **Makes:** `minecraft:charcoal`, `gtceu:creosote_bucket`
- **Stock:** `minecraft:charcoal` in drawer, `gtceu:creosote_bucket` in fluid cell
- **Watch out:**
    - Fan through a lava block = free charcoal, no fuel.
    - Coke ovens are for creosote (treated wood, lubricant), not for charcoal speed.

## Steam engines + generator coils

- **Module key:** `plan:power` · step 7
- **Chain:** [Create steam power: Steam Engines + Generator Coils](../chains/steam/power_steam_engine.md); also [Magnets: from magnetite block to Fluxuated Magnetite](../chains/lv/magnets.md), [Free rotation (Create)](../chains/steam/create_power.md)
- **Uses:** `minecraft:charcoal`
- **Makes:** `create_new_age:generator_coil`, `gtceu:lv_1a_energy_converter`
- **Power:** 3 coils × 12 fluxuated: 1 958 FE/t at 136 RPM, 3 686 at 256 RPM
- **Watch out:**
    - Boiler level = lowest of burners, tank blocks / 4, water / 10 mB/t.
    - Start coils with redstone magnets; upgrade to fluxuated (force 8) once the LV polarizer and bender run.
    - Run the coil shaft at 256 RPM.
