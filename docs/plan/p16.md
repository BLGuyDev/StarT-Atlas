- **Makes:** `gtceu:uv_energy_input_hatch`, `gtceu:uv_electric_motor`, `gtceu:uv_electric_pump`, `gtceu:uv_robot_arm`, `gtceu:uv_field_generator`
- **Power:** Assembly Line 122 880 EU/t, 30 s
- **Watch out:**
