# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1.

## Our pledge
We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive; critique facts and code, not people.
- Accept corrections gracefully; give credit to those who found the answer.
- Keep players' private data private: do not post other people's chat logs, names or screenshots without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, trolling or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and comments that do not follow this code, and may restrict
participation of those who repeatedly violate it. Report problems by opening an issue or contacting a maintainer
privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile.
